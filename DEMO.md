# Demo inputs

All inputs here are fictional test material; no personal information is used.

## Simplify

Paste this into **Make it easier to understand**:

> To maintain access to the community reading room, members must return borrowed materials within fourteen calendar days. A maximum of three items may be checked out concurrently. On Tuesdays the reading room ceases public operations at 17:00. Large-print material can be requested from the front desk.

Expected behavior: a concise plain-language summary that preserves the three-book limit, 14-day duration, Tuesday closing time, and large-print availability.

## Describe image

Select **Describe an image** and upload `public/demo-notice.png`. Expected behavior: objective description of the library notice and its visible text. The image itself was created for this demo.

## Translate

Select **Translate and explain**, choose Hindi, and paste the same text. Expected behavior: a Hindi translation that preserves numbers and timing.

## Read aloud

Use **Read aloud** on a generated answer. The available voice depends on the visitor's device and browser.
